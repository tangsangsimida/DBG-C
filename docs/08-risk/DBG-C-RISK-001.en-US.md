# DBG-C Risk Register

**Document ID:** DBG-C-RISK-001　**Version:** V0.15　**Status:** Risk register; Target overvoltage, Type-C mapping, and PB5 reset-polarity risks added; all remain open

Ratings use qualitative High/Medium/Low and await an agreed project scoring method.

| ID | Risk / trigger | Consequence | Rating | Mitigation | Verification | Status |
